About the map: from 540 to about 2 million years ago the map shows the PALEOMAP paleo-elevation models of Scotese and Wright (2018), one every 5 million years, blended in between. They show where land, shallow seas and mountains were at each time, at 1° resolution. Reconstructions before about 200 million years ago are less certain because the seafloor that recorded plate motions has since been destroyed. After that, the map uses today's coastlines; ice-age sea levels are not drawn. Line widths after 1500 are proportional to the number of people where a source gives a count; dashed branches have no count of their own. Prehistoric routes are generalized paths through dated archaeological and genetic sites, and several dates, especially the first arrivals in Australia and the Americas, are still debated.
